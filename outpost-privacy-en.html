Outpost Defensive Clash Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · 한국어

Outpost Defensive Clash (the "App") respects your privacy and complies with applicable laws. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address, phone number, or location, and it does not require you to create an account.

To process the in-app purchase ("Unlock All Missions"), the following information is sent to our purchase-processing service:

  • A random anonymous identifier generated by the App (not linked to you personally)
  • Purchase history (product, purchase time, receipt data) and purchase verification status
  • Your IP address and device and app version information, which are transmitted automatically when the service is contacted

Payment details such as card numbers are handled directly by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). The App and the purchase-processing service do not receive them. In builds that do not offer purchases (such as a web version), none of the information above is sent.

2. How We Use and Share Information

This information is used only to verify purchases, restore purchases, and prevent fraudulent payments. We do not use it for advertising or marketing and we do not sell it. Purchase processing is handled by the following services:

  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC) — Android
  • Apple App Store payments (Apple Inc.) — iOS
  • RevenueCat, Inc. — purchase verification and purchase status management (Privacy Policy)

We do not provide or share your information with any other third party.

3. How Game Progress Is Stored

Star ratings, best clear times, sound settings, and whether you have seen the help screen are stored only in your device's local storage (Local Storage / app sandbox). They are not sent to any server of ours. The App does not operate its own server. When you uninstall the App, the game data stored on your device is deleted.

If you have enabled your operating system's automatic backup (for example, Android Backup or iCloud Backup), app data may be backed up to that service according to its settings. This is a feature provided by your operating system, and the App does not separately collect it.

4. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics

The App does not include any advertising network SDK or behavioral analytics tool, and it does not track you. The only SDK it includes is the purchase-processing SDK described above.

5. Retention and Deletion

You can delete the game data stored on your device by uninstalling the App. To request deletion of purchase-related information held by the purchase-processing service, contact us using the details below. Transaction records that must be kept by law may be retained for the legally required period.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personally identifiable information from any user, including children under 14. In-app purchases are subject to the age and payment policies of Google Play or the Apple App Store.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page.
